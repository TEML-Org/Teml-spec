What's new in v-alpha-002

Each tab shows one new feature as a small model, then everything together in the hotel model. Every board is drawn directly from the TEML shown under it. Click any sticky or slice heading for its details and Given/When/Then specs.

Revised after review: external systems no longer put events into our model. A system calls our API (for example, a webhook), which issues one of our commands. The events that follow are ours and can trigger our workflows.
